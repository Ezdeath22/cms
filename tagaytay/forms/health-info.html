Health Services Information

The Tagaytay City Health Office provides various services to residents. For the latest schedules, announcements, and specific program details, please refer to the following resources:

  • Visit the Official Tagaytay City Website for announcements.
  • Contact the City Health Office directly at [Insert CHO Phone Number Here].
  • Visit your nearest Barangay Health Center for local schedules and services.

Services may include:

  • Consultations
  • Vaccinations (Routine & Special Programs)
  • Family Planning
  • Prenatal Care
  • Tuberculosis (TB) DOTS
  • Dental Services (check availability)
  • Medical Missions (announced periodically)

Information here is for general guidance only. Please verify directly with the City Health Office or your Barangay Health Center.
