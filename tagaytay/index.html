Welcome to Tagaytay City

Nestled along a ridge overlooking the majestic Taal Volcano and Lake, Tagaytay City is a premier tourist destination in the Philippines, renowned for its cool climate, breathtaking vistas, and vibrant atmosphere. Just a short drive from Metro Manila, it offers a refreshing highland escape.

A Glimpse into History

The name "Tagaytay" is believed to derive from the Tagalog words "taga" (to cut) and "itay" (father), echoing a local legend of a dramatic hunt. During revolutionary times, its ridges provided strategic cover. Established as a chartered city on June 21, 1938, Tagaytay was envisioned as a key tourism zone, a vision it fulfills today as a hub for leisure, dining, and agriculture.
